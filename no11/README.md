## 引用与说明

- Ersner-Hershfield, H., Wimmer, G. E., & Knutson, B. (2009). Saving for the future self: Neural measures of future self-continuity predict temporal discounting. *Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience*, 4(1), 85–92. 想到未来的自己时，相关脑区的活动更接近想到他人；这种差异越大，越偏好眼前的小奖励。
